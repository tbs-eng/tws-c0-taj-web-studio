After launch

The care plan.

A launch is a snapshot. Addresses change, listings drift, staff move on, and reviews stop arriving — usually without anyone noticing until a family or a referral source does. The care plan is the monthly work that keeps the site current and the records behind it accurate.

Care plan

From $300/mo

  • Your contact form tested with a real message — and the spam log checked for enquiries that were silently discarded
  • Your Google listing checked — hours, address, phone. The one thing that changes without anyone asking you
  • Reviews asked for, tracked and responded to — the part that compounds
  • Your public records re-checked, and corrections confirmed where we've filed them
  • Site, certificate and email still resolving
  • A set block of small content edits each month
  • A plain-language report: what changed, what it means

What sets the price

  • A site I built. The simplest to keep accurate, and the lowest monthly.
  • A site I inherit. More surface to check and more places for records to drift, so it costs more to keep right.
  • How much of the record you want covered — one location and one listing, or a full set of directory and enrollment records.

I quote it after the build, once I can see what keeping it accurate actually takes.

Month-to-month — cancel anytime with 30 days' notice. Reviews and records compound slowly; three to six months is where it starts to show.
