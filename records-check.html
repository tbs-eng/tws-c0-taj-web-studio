Why this exists

Families and referral sources check your records before they check your website.

A discharge planner looking for a hospice, or a daughter at nine at night, doesn't start on your home page. They land on a directory that copied its information from your federal record — and if that record says something out of date, so does everything downstream of it.

Almost nobody has read their own. It costs nothing to look, it takes a specialist about a day, and in three checks so far I have not found a provider whose records all agreed.

What I check

Seven checks, all on public records.

01

Your federal record

Legal name, any additional business names, mailing and practice address, the person named as authorized official.

02

The name on your sign

Whether the name you actually trade under matches the one on the federal record. This is the one most often wrong.

03

Your Medicare address

Whether the mailing address on your enrollment file matches your office — and where it has already been copied to.

04

Who runs the place

Whether three independent directories name the same person as running your organization. Often they don't.

05

Whether anyone links to you

How many of your directory listings carry your website address at all, and whether they use the secure version.

06

What the AI assistants show

Whether ChatGPT and Gemini describe you using your own reviews or a third party's — and whether the two agree.

07

Your opening hours

A 24-hour service whose Google profile says it closes at 5:30 is losing the nine-o'clock call. Free to fix, once you know.

The part that matters

Two of the five are things I can't fix and won't charge you for.

Your federal record and your Medicare enrollment file sit inside government systems. Only you — or whoever handles your enrollment paperwork — can change them. Most are free and take about ten minutes once you know they're there.

I tell you anyway, and I say plainly which ones they are. A salesperson finds five problems they happen to sell the cure for. That difference is the whole reason this is worth paying for.
